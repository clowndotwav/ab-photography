About AB

Chasing things
that don't hold still.

I'm Adam Bridge. I point long glass at things that move too fast — birds of prey in the Perth hills, ice hockey under arena lights, and anything with an engine and a racing line.

The three genres look different but they're the same problem: you get one frame, the light is never what you'd order, and the moment doesn't come back. Wildlife taught me patience. Motorsport taught me timing. Ice hockey taught me to shoot at ISO 6400 and stop apologising for it.

Based in Western Australia, available Australia-wide for race weekends, seasons, and anything that needs someone comfortable in the cold, the rain or the gravel trap.
